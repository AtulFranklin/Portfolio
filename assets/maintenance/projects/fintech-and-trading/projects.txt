title: Trade on Paper
year: 2026
image: trade-on-paper.jpg
blurb: I co-founded INVA Capital Markets and built this as our strategy testbed, a full paper-trading simulator for NSE/BSE equities and F&O, so I could practise without risking money.
points:
- 5x intraday leverage, margin tracking, stop loss / target and auto square-off at 3:20 PM
- Yahoo, Stooq and NSE data fallback chain so prices keep flowing when a source fails
- Black-Scholes option chain with delta, PCR and max pain for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and FINNIFTY
- Automated scalping engine with RSI / volume confirmation, tick-based reversal detection and daily stop limits
- Momentum models (weighted 1W-6M score, regime classifier needing 3 of 5 signals) screen 50 F&O stocks, with dashboards for scans and P&L
- SQL databases to analyze 100+ simulated option trades, Excel trade-log export and Google Sign-In with per-user data
tags: Flutter, Dart, Firebase, SQL, Quant

title: Trade Order Matching Simulator
year: 2025
image: order-matching-simulator.jpg
blurb: A tiny stock exchange core in plain Java. Buy and sell orders go in, trades come out, matched by price-time priority.
points:
- Limit and market orders, partial fills, cancels, and trades at the resting order's price

- 60 plain-Java test checks covering FIFO priority, market sweeps and bad input
